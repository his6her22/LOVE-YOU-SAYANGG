A Letter From My Heart

My Sayang, my Habibti, my little Mochi —

There are so many words in so many languages, and still none of them feel big enough to hold what I feel for you. You walked into my world and made everything softer, warmer, brighter — the way sunlight feels on your face on a quiet morning.

I find myself getting lost in your eyes every single time — those gorgeous, deep, completely unfair eyes that somehow make everything else fade away. And your smile? The moment it appears it's already spreading to everyone around you. I swear it has its own gravity.

And don't even get me started on your nose — the cutest nose in the entire world, with those little nostrils that make the most adorable cat ears. I noticed it and I will never stop thinking it's the most delightfully perfect thing. You are, genuinely, one of a kind.

You are the first person I want to tell things to. The last face I think about before I sleep. I don't just love you — I admire you. Your kindness, your laugh, the way your mind works. Thank you for being exactly who you are. Every single day, I count myself lucky.

— forever yours 🌹
