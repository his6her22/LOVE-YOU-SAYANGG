A Letter From My Soul

My Sayang, my Habibti, my little Mochi —

I don't think words will ever be enough to tell you how much I love you — but I will spend every single day of my life trying.

You are amazing. Not in the way people casually throw that word around — truly, deeply, remarkably amazing. The way you carry yourself, the way you love, the way you face everything life throws at you with a grace that leaves me in awe every single time.

I fell in love with you — your personality, your soul, your strength. The strength you have is something I admire more than you will ever know. It is one of the most beautiful things about you, and it makes me love you more every single day.

You make my days brighter. A single smile from you and the whole world feels lighter. You are the sun to my moon — without you, I am just darkness. With you, I glow.

🌸 ✨ 🌸 Meeting you, loving you — these are my biggest achievements. But the moment I am most proud of, the moment I will carry in my heart forever, is the moment I asked you to be my wife. That was the best thing I have ever done.

My biggest dream is simple — to see you happy. That is all I want. Every prayer I make, every dua I raise to Allah, is for you. I make dua that I become someone you are proud of. Someone worthy of you. Someone you are glad to call yours.

May Allah grant us the support of one another — in this dunya and in the akhira. May He keep our hearts tied together, our love protected, and our path lit. Ameen.

There is no one else in this entire world I would choose. It has always been you. It will always be you.

I LOVE YOU, SAYANG —
with all my heart and all my soul. 💖

— forever and always, yours 🌙
